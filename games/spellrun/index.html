Spellrun

A real-time multiplayer typing race where words are fuel, not a progress bar.

Android version in soft launch. Free to play.

About Spellrun

Everyone runs at the same pace until someone earns the right to run faster.

Features

  • Typing is not your speedometer

    Every runner shares one base pace. Raw words per minute does not push you forward — keeping a clean streak alive does. Drop the streak and you drop back to the pack.

  • Words are fuel for spells

    Completed words charge MP. Spend it to dash ahead, or to freeze the runner who just passed you. The race is decided by what you cast and when, not by who has the fastest fingers.

  • A ladder worth climbing

    Every race moves your rating. Climb from Bronze through Silver, Gold, Platinum, Diamond and Master to Grandmaster — matched against players near your own rank the whole way up.

  • Settled on the server

    Distance, finish order, placement and rating are all calculated server-side. The result you see is the result everyone else sees, which is what makes the ladder mean anything.
