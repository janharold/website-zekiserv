Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 23, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Zekiserv ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and shares information when you use our mobile applications sumscan and scansum (together, the "Apps"). By using either App, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

Information We Collect

Depending on how you use sumscan or scansum, we may collect:

  • Information you provide — such as account details, preferences, or content you create or upload within an App.
  • Device and usage information — such as device type, operating system, app version, language settings, and general interaction data (for example, features used and session duration).
  • Camera and scan data — if you use scanning features, images or documents you capture may be processed on your device and, where necessary to provide the service, transmitted securely to perform recognition or storage functions you request.
  • Diagnostic data — such as crash logs or performance reports to help us maintain and improve the Apps.

How We Use Information

We use collected information to:

  • Provide, operate, and maintain sumscan and scansum;
  • Improve features, reliability, and user experience;
  • Respond to support requests and communicate important service updates;
  • Monitor for abuse, fraud, or security issues; and
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations.

Legal Bases for Processing

Where required by law, we process personal data based on one or more of the following: your consent, performance of a contract with you, our legitimate interests in operating and improving the Apps, or compliance with legal obligations.

Sharing with Third Parties

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information with:

  • Service providers — such as hosting, analytics, crash reporting, or customer support vendors that process data on our behalf under contractual safeguards.
  • Platform providers — such as Apple or Google, as required for app distribution, in-app purchases, or platform services you choose to use.
  • Legal and safety purposes — when we believe disclosure is necessary to comply with law, enforce our terms, or protect the rights, property, or safety of users or others.

Data Retention

We retain information only for as long as needed to provide the Apps, fulfill the purposes described in this policy, resolve disputes, enforce agreements, and meet legal requirements. When data is no longer needed, we delete or anonymize it in accordance with our retention practices.

Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures designed to protect information against unauthorized access, loss, or misuse. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your Choices and Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict processing of your personal information, or to withdraw consent where processing is consent-based. You can also adjust certain permissions (such as camera or notifications) in your device settings. To exercise applicable rights, contact us using the details below.

Children's Privacy

sumscan and scansum are not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, please contact us and we will take appropriate steps to delete it.

International Transfers

If you use the Apps from outside the country where our servers or service providers are located, your information may be transferred to and processed in other countries that may have different data protection laws. Where required, we implement appropriate safeguards for such transfers.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date above and, where appropriate, provide additional notice within the Apps or on this page. Continued use of sumscan or scansum after changes become effective constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
