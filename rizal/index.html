Rizal

A quiet place to write.

A minimalist Markdown editor crafted for macOS. Live formatting renders in place, ambient soundscapes ease your concentration, and your words stay strictly on your local disk as plain text.

Download on Mac App Store Support & Help

Engineered for Writing Flow

A companion that gets out of your way, keeping your attention on the paragraph in front of you.

✍️

Live Markdown

Format headings, quotes, bold, and lists seamlessly. Markdown markup stays hidden while reading and only reveals itself on the line your caret touches.

🕯️

Manila & Lamplight

Two artisanal palettes designed for writers. Manila paper with iron-gall ink blue for daytime clarity; lamplight warm black with an amber caret for nocturnal focus.

🌧️

Generative Audio Mixer

Ambient soundscapes (rain, fire, wind, night) synthesized live in Web Audio. Mix your atmosphere and pair it with tactile typewriter, mechanical, or soft key clicks.

🎯

Typewriter Focus

Keep your active cursor vertically centered as your thoughts flow. Dim surrounding paragraphs or lines to keep your current thought front and center.

📂

Local-First Library

Your notes belong to you. Saved directly to your Mac as standard .md files. No database, zero cloud lock-in, and instant compatibility with iCloud Drive and Git.

🔒

Private by Architecture

No telemetry, no tracking analytics, no user accounts, and zero remote storage. Rizal operates entirely offline on your Mac.

System Requirements: Requires macOS 11.0 Big Sur or later. Built as a universal binary optimized for Apple Silicon (M1/M2/M3/M4) and Intel Macs.
