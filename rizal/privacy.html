Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 23, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Zekiserv ("we," "us," or "our") handles information when you use our desktop application Rizal (the "App") on macOS. By using the App, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

Summary

  • We do not collect personal information. We do not ask for, receive, store, or transmit your name, email, account details, or identity.
  • Zero telemetry and tracking. The App contains no advertising SDKs, tracking pixels, or third-party analytics scripts.
  • Your notes remain 100% on your device. Every document you write is saved directly to your local file system as plain Markdown (.md) files under your control. We never upload your notes to any server.
  • Offline-first by design. Rizal functions completely offline without requiring an internet connection or account sign-in.

Information We Do Not Collect

Rizal is built as a quiet, private writing environment. We do not operate user accounts, cloud databases, sync servers, or usage-tracking telemetry pipelines.

We do not collect:

  • Note contents, titles, metadata, tags, or file structures;
  • Personal information such as your name, email address, or billing details;
  • Device identifiers, IP addresses, or browsing history;
  • Typing metrics, keystroke logs, or usage duration logs.

Where Your Data Is Stored

All notes, library folders, and app preferences (such as your chosen theme, active focus mode, and sound mixer settings) are stored locally on your Mac.

Your notes reside in your designated library folder (by default inside your Documents directory, or any custom folder you choose). You retain full ownership and direct access to your files at all times through macOS Finder. Because your files remain strictly local, we cannot read, restore, or delete them for you.

File System Permissions

To open and save your markdown notes, Rizal requests standard macOS file access permissions to your selected library folder. Granting this permission enables the App to read and write files locally. It does not transmit your files or directory contents to Zekiserv or any third party.

Audio Synthesis

Rizal's ambient sounds (rain, fire, wind, night) and keystroke audio effects are generated entirely in real-time on your computer using the Web Audio API. No audio recordings or microphone inputs are captured, processed, or uploaded.

Third Parties & App Store Distribution

Rizal is distributed through the Apple Mac App Store. When you download, update, or purchase the App, Apple may collect data according to its own privacy policy. We do not receive your payment credentials or Apple ID.

Children's Privacy

Rizal does not address or collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

If we make changes to this Privacy Policy, the revised version will be published on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
