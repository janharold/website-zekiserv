Frequently Asked Questions

Where are my notes saved?

Your notes are saved as standard plain-text Markdown files (.md) directly in the folder you selected during setup (by default, a Rizal folder inside your macOS Documents directory). You can open, copy, or back them up using Finder at any time.

Can I sync notes across devices?

Yes! Because Rizal stores plain Markdown files on disk, you can select an iCloud Drive, Dropbox, or OneDrive folder as your library directory. Any cloud provider that syncs macOS folders will automatically keep your files synchronized.

How does note auto-naming work?

When you create a new note, it starts as Untitled. As soon as you type the first line, Rizal automatically names the file after your opening phrase (up to 50 characters). Once you manually rename a note in the sidebar, Rizal locks your custom title and stops auto-naming that note.

How do I customize the ambient sounds and keyboard effects?

Click the sound mixer icon in the toolbar. You can independently toggle and adjust volume sliders for atmospheric ambient layers (Rain, Fire, Wind, Night) and tactile typing sound packs (Typewriter with carriage bell, Mechanical, or Soft).

How do I toggle Focus Mode?

Focus Mode keeps your active cursor vertically centered (typewriter scrolling) and softly dims surrounding text. Click the focus indicator in the status bar to toggle it, or right-click the dot to switch between line-dimming and paragraph-dimming.

Does Rizal work without an internet connection?

Yes, 100%. Rizal has no cloud dependencies, requires no account sign-in, and all audio synthesis is computed locally on your device.
